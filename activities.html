Practice debates

Weekly · casual Lincoln-Douglas · open to all

We run casual Lincoln-Douglas rounds: one debater against one, on a values question rather than a policy plan. Rounds are run for feedback and no record is kept.

You get notes on your one-on-one debating and on how you work with a partner when we run team formats.

Topics come from the running list. If you want to argue something that is not on it, say so.

Masterclasses

On request · one skill at a time

Stuck on one aspect of public speaking? Request a masterclass and we will build a workshop on that skill.

Keep the request narrow: filler words, running out of air, freezing on rebuttal, reading off the page, speeding up when nervous, structuring a point under time pressure.

No minimum group size and no form. Tell an officer what the problem is, or post it in the Classroom.
