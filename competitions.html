ReconX, HTHS, and outside competitions

We prepare you to enter as an individual. Read the note below before you plan around anything on this page.

Read this first

We do not represent High Tech

The club does not send an official school team to outside competitions and does not represent High Technology High School at them. What follows are suggestions: competitions worth entering, which we will help you prepare for as an individual.

We provide practice rounds, feedback on your cases, and a walkthrough of the format. Registration, transport, chaperones, and entering under the school’s name are yours to arrange.

HTHS debate competitions

In-school · through the year

Competitions held inside High Tech. The easiest entry point if you have never competed: same format as the practice rounds, same people, no travel.

Dates go up in the Google Classroom as they are set.

Outside circuit competitions

Individual entry · you register

If you find a tournament you want to enter, bring it to an officer. We will work the format with you, run practice rounds, and give feedback on your cases.

Registration, fees, and travel are yours to handle. Start early; formats differ enough that a few sessions help.

The formats guide covers what each tournament is asking for.
