Why join

What you get better at

Debate trains what classes already grade you on. You do not need to have done it before, and you do not need to want to compete.

Clarity

Convey ideas effectively

Say what you mean in an order people can follow.

Presence

Converse with bravado

Hold a position out loud instead of hedging it.

Speed

Build arguments quickly

Put a case together in minutes. Presentations, interviews, and oral exams all ask for the same thing.
