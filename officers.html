On experience

You do not have to be a debater

Worth saying plainly, since it is the thing that stops most people walking in: the president is not really a debater either.

This club is not built for people who already compete. It is built for people who want to get better at conveying an idea, holding a conversation with some conviction behind it, and putting an argument together faster than they currently can. Competing is one option among several, and most members never do it.

If you have been putting off coming because you assumed everyone else would be experienced — they are not, and the officers are running casual rounds precisely so that being new is not a problem.
