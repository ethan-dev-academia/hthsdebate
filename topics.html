Discussion topics

Drug policy, taxes, guns, and more

The running list for practice rounds. Sides are assigned, and often not the one you hold.

TopicRoughly about
Drug legalization and decriminalizationWhere the line between the two sits, and what each is meant to fix.
Student loan forgivenessWho is owed what, and the effect on students who come after.
Free and universal healthcareHealthcare as a right against healthcare as a market, and the cost of each.
Flat tax vs. progressive taxFairness against simplicity.
Gun controlRights, risk, and which restrictions hold up against evidence.
Police reformWhat reform means concretely, and which version is being argued for.
ElectionsHow they are run, who they represent, and what a different system would trade away.
And moreThe list is not fixed.
